Let it ring through

Android needs your OK before an app can ring over the lock screen on time.

  • NotificationsSo a reminder can ring at allAllowed
  • Full-screen callsRing over the lock screen like a call
  • Alarms & remindersRing at 9:30, not somewhere near itAllowed
  • CalendarRead events from every calendar on the phoneAllowed
  • ContactsFind birthdays saved in contacts

3 of 5 allowed. You can change these later in Settings.
